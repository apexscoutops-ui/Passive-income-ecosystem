What this means

These are rough estimates, not guarantees. They assume water heating is about 18% of your bill and apply savings ranges based on U.S. Department of Energy guidance (24–34% for low hot water use, 8–14% for high use). The DOE doesn't publish separate figures by fuel type, so our electric estimates are a conservative adjustment of our own. Your actual savings depend on how much hot water you use, local energy prices, your current heater, and installation costs.
